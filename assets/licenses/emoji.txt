Emoji catalog, names and search keywords

Unicode Emoji 16.0, fully-qualified emoji and skin-tone sequences.
https://www.unicode.org/Public/emoji/16.0/emoji-test.txt
Adapted into EmojiCatalog.kt: base emoji grouped for Aster and supported skin-tone variants.

Unicode CLDR release-48, English and Romanian annotations and annotationsDerived.
https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr/tree/release-48/common
Adapted into search.tsv: selected Aster emoji, names and combined keywords.
The data is bundled; the app never downloads it.
